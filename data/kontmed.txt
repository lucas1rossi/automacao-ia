Kontour Cranial Fixation and Reparation Systems
Made from British Invibio® PEEK-OPTIMA® polymer, a high-performance biomaterial that meets the strictest implant standards. It features excellent mechanical properties, ease of shaping, chemical stability, and biocompatibility. It can withstand high-temperature steam and radiation sterilization and has radiolucency, making it widely used in cardiovascular, dental, neurosurgery, maxillofacial surgery, and orthopedic implants.
