Safety IV Cannula

Advanced Needlestick Protection for Safer IV Therapy

The 
SPM Safety IV Cannula
 is a sterile, single-use peripheral intravenous catheter engineered to provide reliable vascular access while enhancing healthcare worker safety through an integrated needle protection mechanism.

Designed for the administration of intravenous fluids, medications, blood products, and blood sampling procedures, the Safety IV Cannula combines superior clinical performance with advanced infection prevention and needlestick injury protection.

Manufactured from premium medical-grade materials and sterilized using Ethylene Oxide (EO), the device is available in multiple configurations including 
with or without wings
 and 
with or without injection port
, making it suitable for a wide range of clinical applications.

Product Overview

The Safety IV Cannula features a precision-engineered stainless-steel introducer needle and a flexible Polyurethane (PUR) or FEP catheter that enables smooth insertion and reliable venous access.

Once vascular access is achieved, the integrated safety mechanism automatically shields the needle upon withdrawal, helping reduce accidental needlestick injuries and improving compliance with modern healthcare safety standards.

The device is color-coded according to gauge size for quick identification and easy selection in busy clinical environments.

Key Features

Integrated Safety Mechanism

Automatic needle protection system helps reduce accidental needlestick injuries during and after cannulation.

Superior Catheter Performance

Flexible PUR/FEP catheter provides excellent kink resistance, patient comfort, and smooth advancement into the vein.

Fast Flashback Confirmation

Transparent flashback chamber enables rapid visualization of blood return for accurate venous access confirmation.

Hydrophobic Filter Technology

Allows air venting while minimizing blood leakage and maintaining procedural cleanliness.

DESCRIPTION:

The SPM’s In-line arterial Blood sampling Kit range is an In-line Arterial Blood Sampling Kit syringes

are used for blood collection by manual aspiration and are supplied without needles. They are

designed to be used for arterial blood collection from an arterial line.

Once the sample is obtained, care is taken to eliminate visible gas bubbles, as these bubbles can

dissolve into the sample and cause inaccurate results. The sealed syringe is taken to a blood gas

analyser. If a plastic blood gas syringe is used, the sample should be transported and kept at room

temperature and analysed within 30 min. The SPM’s “In-line arterial Blood sampling Kit is Gamma

Irradiation sterilized. Sterilized using Gamma Irradiation in accordance with ISO 11137, SAL 10⁻⁶.

INTENDED PURPOSE:

In-line Arterial Blood Sampling Kit designed to obtain an in-line arterial blood

specimen while maintaining a closed system.

INDICATIONS FOR USE

The In-line Arterial Blood Sampling Kit is designed for arterial blood specimen collection for further

blood gas analysis.

INTENDED PATIENT POPULATION:

INTENDED USER:

In-line Arterial Blood Sampling Kit is intended to be used by the trained healthcare professionals like

The SPM’s In-line arterial Blood sampling Kit is a sterile, single-use luer lock syringe without needle is

intended to use for arterial blood sampling. The air is removed from the syringe and put the luer cap

variants also contains hydrophobic filter which help to completely remove air bubble filter.

POTENTIAL COMPLICATIONS:

INSTRUCTIONS FOR USE:

• Step 1. Take pre heparinized Arterial Blood Collection Kit 1ml or 3ml as required for blood

sample.

• Step 2. Collect 1ml or 3ml arterial blood in this heparinized syringe (filling the syringe

completely is very important).

• Step 3. Remove air bubbles quickly (if any), and place the luer cap. Mix the blood in the syringe

gently by rotating the syringe between the palms of the hands to ensure mixing of blood with

heparin.

• Step 4. Send the sample to the laboratory immediately. If it is not possible to send immediately

then keep the syringe in a container having ice-water slurry (not with ice alone) but not for too

long because the plastic syringes are partially gas permeable and this permeability increases at
